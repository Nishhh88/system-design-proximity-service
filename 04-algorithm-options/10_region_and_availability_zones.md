# Region and Availability Zones

Location based service is deployed to multiple regions and availability zones

* This makes the users physically closer to the system.
* Gives the flexibility to spread the traffic evenly across the population. Depending upon the population density, some countries should be put in separate regions like China and India, or we can even deploy location-based services in multiple availability zopnes to spread the load.
* Some of the countries might have privacy laws that would require the user data to be used and stored locally. In this case, a region in that country could we setup and DNS routing could be deployed to restrict all requests from country to only that region.
